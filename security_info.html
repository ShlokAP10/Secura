SECURITY, EXPLAINED

Your password never sits in plain text.

Secura is designed to demonstrate the key protections that make account sign-in safer.

⌑ Security-first design
⌁
PASSWORD PROTECTION

bcrypt hashing

Passwords are salted and transformed into one-way hashes before they are stored.

⊘
BRUTE-FORCE DEFENSE

5-attempt lockout

Five consecutive failed sign-ins pause access for five minutes.

◈
SESSION SAFETY

Protected sessions

Authenticated areas require a valid server-side session and CSRF-protected forms.

HOW WE PROTECT YOUR DATA

Clear rules, explained simply.

Security should be understandable—not hidden behind technical language.

◌
PASSWORD POLICY

Strong by default

Passwords need at least 8 characters, plus uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a symbol.

◷
LOCKOUT RULE

Five attempts, five minutes

Repeated failed sign-ins pause access temporarily to slow down brute-force attacks.

⌑
PRIVACY NOTE

No plaintext passwords

We store your account details and a one-way password hash—never your original password.
